\paragraph{Clean and adversarial MAS comparison.}
Tables~\ref{tab:appendix-deepseek-clean-raac-mca}
and~\ref{tab:appendix-qwen-clean-raac-mca} report results under the
\DeepSeekModelName{} and \QwenModelName{} backbones, respectively. Each table
compares the Clean MAS setting with the RAAC and MCA adversarial settings
under the same question sets and evaluation rule. Clean MAS achieves the
highest mean accuracy on all four datasets for both backbones. Both
adversarial settings reduce accuracy, with the magnitude of the reduction
varying across models and datasets.

\paragraph{Attack comparison and dataset sensitivity.}
RAAC yields lower accuracy than MCA for every backbone--dataset combination,
indicating that strategy~13 causes a stronger degradation than the baseline-1
attack. The effect is also dataset dependent. For \DeepSeekModelName{}, the
largest Clean-to-RAAC drops occur on MMLU (23.33 points) and MedMCQA
(22.84 points). For \QwenModelName{}, MedMCQA exhibits the largest reduction
(18.34 points), while SCALR is comparatively more robust (6.00 points).
These results suggest that both the backbone model and the reasoning domain
affect adversarial susceptibility in multi-agent deliberation.
